Terms & disclaimer

Preparation, not adjudication.

ClearedLife is designed as an organizational and educational tool. It does not grant, sponsor, adjudicate, guarantee, or predict a U.S. Government security clearance.

Official guidance controls

Users should follow instructions from their employer, Facility Security Officer, Security Officer, sponsoring agency, and official government sources. Clearance status questions should be directed to the responsible security organization.

No legal advice

ClearedLife is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Denials, revocations, Statements of Reasons, criminal matters, and other high-stakes adjudicative issues should be handled with qualified counsel as appropriate.

No concealment coaching

The service should never be used to omit, conceal, falsify, or manipulate information requested by the government. The product philosophy is accurate disclosure, clear explanation, and reliable documentation.

Independent service

ClearedLife is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by DCSA or the United States Government.
