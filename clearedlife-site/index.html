Life changes.
Your records should too.

Cleared personnel can have ongoing reporting responsibilities. ClearedLife is designed to help you recognize a potential reporting event, document it, and route you back to the right security contact—not tell you what to hide.

Disclose accurately. Explain clearly. Document everything.

ClearedLife does not make adjudicative decisions and does not replace your FSO, security officer, agency guidance, or legal counsel.

Trust by design

The product should earn the right
to hold sensitive information.

A production ClearedLife Vault should be built with strong encryption, minimal data collection, explicit retention controls, auditability, and a clear threat model before storing sensitive personnel-vetting history.

OUR PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
  1. 01

    Preparation, not prediction. We score readiness—not adjudicative outcomes.

  2. 02

    Accuracy over avoidance. We never coach concealment or omission.

  3. 03

    Route to authority. Official questions go back to the responsible security office or agency.

  4. 04

    Human help when stakes rise. Legal and complex adjudicative matters belong with qualified counsel.
